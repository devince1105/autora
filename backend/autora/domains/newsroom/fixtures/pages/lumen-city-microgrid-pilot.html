Lumen City switches on its first neighbourhood solar microgrid

By a fictional reporter · 15 September 2026

Lumen City switched on its first neighbourhood solar microgrid on Monday. The pilot in Harbor District links 1,200 rooftop solar panels with a 4 MWh battery.

The city says the microgrid can keep about 3,000 homes powered for six hours during a grid outage.

"This is the first of five districts," said Mira Chen, the city's energy commissioner. "We will decide on the next one after a year of data."

The project cost NT$420 million to build, according to the city's budget office.
