What a neighbourhood microgrid really costs

An independent review of Lumen City's Harbor District microgrid puts its cost at NT$4.6 billion over ten years once maintenance and battery replacement are counted.

The review warns that the battery's capacity fades by about 2% a year, so the six-hour backup the city promises would fall to under five hours by the end of the decade.

The city disputes the estimate and says maintenance is already in its NT$420 million build budget.
